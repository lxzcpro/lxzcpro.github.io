Hi! I am **Xuting Zhang** (pronounced *Shyoo-Ting Jahng*), a final-year **Biological Science** undergraduate at [Westlake University](https://en.westlake.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. [Jianyang Zeng](https://en.westlake.edu.cn/faculty/jianyang-zeng.html). Currently, I am a visiting undergraduate research fellow at Harvard University's [Department of Stem Cell and Regenerative Biology](https://hscrb.harvard.edu/) and the [Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard](https://www.broadinstitute.org/), under the supervision of Prof. [Jason Buenrostro](https://www.broadinstitute.org/bios/jason-d-buenrostro). I was also an exchange student at [Duke University](https://duke.edu/) in Fall 2025.

I develop interpretable machine learning methods to infer gene regulatory networks and study disease-associated tissue organization from **single-cell and spatial multi-omics** data. My previous research includes **protein language models** with Prof. [Fajie Yuan](https://fajieyuan.github.io/) and protein–ligand binding and **virtual screening** with Prof. [Rohit Singh](https://biostat.duke.edu/profile/rohit-singh).
