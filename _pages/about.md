Hi! I am **Xuting Zhang**, a final-year **Biological Science** undergraduate at [Westlake University](https://en.westlake.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. [Jianyang Zeng](https://en.westlake.edu.cn/faculty/jianyang-zeng.html). Currently, I am a visiting undergraduate research fellow at the [Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard](https://www.broadinstitute.org/) and in the [Department of Stem Cell and Regenerative Biology at Harvard University](https://hscrb.harvard.edu/), under the supervision of Prof. [Jason Buenrostro](https://www.broadinstitute.org/bios/jason-d-buenrostro) and Dr. [Ruochi Zhang](https://ruochiz.com/). I was also an exchange student at [Duke University](https://duke.edu/) in Fall 2025.

Broadly, my research interests lie at the intersection of **machine learning and the life sciences**. I am particularly interested in interpretable approaches for studying gene regulation and disease-associated tissue organization using **single-cell and spatial multi-omics** data. Previously, I worked on **protein language models** with Prof. [Fajie Yuan](https://fajieyuan.github.io/) and on protein–ligand binding and **virtual screening** with Prof. [Rohit Singh](https://biostat.duke.edu/profile/rohit-singh).
